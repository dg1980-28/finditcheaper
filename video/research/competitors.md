# Competitor scan

## 12 Oct 2026 (first pass, websites only; Chrome was offline so TikTok accounts not yet done)

Source: public Similarweb pages, Google results for "medicube collagen balm cheapest price uk".

### What ranks for "<product> cheapest price uk"
- Local newspaper "recommended" sections (Sussex Express, Portsmouth News, NationalWorld) with news-style headlines: "Viral K-beauty brand Medicube cuts price of collagen skincare bundle by 40%". Affiliate content riding on the newspaper's domain authority. We can't copy the authority, but the headline format is copyable: brand + "cuts price" / "is £X cheaper at" + the number.
- Cosmetify (beauty price comparison). 337K visits per quarter, but 32% of it is paid search and it has 7 referrers. It buys traffic. Not our model.
- Retailers themselves (Cult Beauty, Superdrug).

### The deal communities
- hotukdeals.com: 20M visits per quarter, 69% direct, then organic search, then email. 19K ranking keywords, mostly brand ("hotukdeals", "hukd", "daily deals site"). 3.95 pages per visit, nearly 5 minutes per visit, 31% bounce. Social traffic comes from YouTube first, then Reddit and Facebook. 94% UK.
- latestdeals.co.uk: 33% direct, then organic, then email. 4.5K keywords ("daily deals site", "discount codes", "latest deals uk"). Social from Facebook and Reddit only. 51% bounce.

### What this says
1. The big deal sites run on habit (direct) and email, not on ranking for product searches. They are not competing with us for "X cheapest price uk"; the newspapers are.
2. YouTube and Reddit are the social channels that actually send traffic to deal sites. TikTok doesn't appear in their social sources at all, which means TikTok views need to convert through the link in bio and the site, not through TikTok sending people on.
3. Nobody in the niche has a clean "A vs B, here's the gap" page per product. That's our gap to own.

### Changes to make
1. Product page titles and H1s in the newspaper style: "Medicube collagen balm is £8 cheaper on Amazon than Superdrug" rather than "Medicube collagen balm: cheapest price in the UK". Both the search phrase and the number in the title.
2. Add an email capture to the site (a one line "deals of the week" sign up). The two biggest sites in the niche both have email as their third channel. Cheap to run from the repo with a free form service later.
3. Post the strongest weekly deal on Reddit (r/UKDeals, r/AmazonUK) and HotUKDeals as a member, linking to our page. Once a week, only when the gap is real.
4. Prioritise YouTube Shorts as the second channel over Instagram, because YouTube is the social source that actually sends traffic in this niche.
